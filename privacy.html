About this sample policyThis template is for general information, not legal advice. A live business should publish an accurate policy reviewed for its services, vendors, and applicable privacy laws.

1. Information used in this website demo

This is a static front-end demonstration with no server-side account system or dispatch service. Features may save sample profile, request, contact-form, admin-preview, theme, or display-preference data in browser storage on the device and browser you use.

That local demo information is not automatically sent to a RoadRescue backend by this template. A person with access to your device and browser profile may be able to view or change it, so do not enter sensitive personal, payment, or account information.

2. Forms, contact, and dispatch

Submitting a request, registration, sign-in, or contact form in this demo does not create a live account, send an email, contact a dispatcher, or place a service order. Demo confirmations are generated in the browser only.

If you contact a real provider by phone or another channel, the information you share is handled through that channel. For urgent situations, contact local emergency services rather than relying on this website demo.

3. Browser storage and your choices

Browser storage allows the demo to remember sample records and interface preferences between visits. It is specific to the browser and device; it is not a cloud backup and may be unavailable in private browsing or after browser data is cleared.

  • To remove demo information, clear this site’s stored data in your browser’s privacy or site settings.
  • Clearing site data may also reset saved demo requests, profiles, and preferences.
  • Use a shared device? Sign out of demo views and clear site data when finished.

4. External resources and providers

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and CSS libraries and icons from jsDelivr. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details. Their handling is governed by their own policies.

Before launching, review whether these integrations are appropriate for your users and region, and document all analytics, hosting, payment, communication, and service-provider tools your live site uses.

5. If you operate a live service

A production RoadRescue service may handle location, contact, vehicle, payment, or service-history information. This demo does not define how a real business collects or uses that information. Before collecting it, explain the purposes and legal basis, what is required, who receives it, where it is stored, and how long it is kept.

6. Security and retention

Local demo data is kept by the browser until it is removed or browser settings clear it; this template does not provide centralized retention controls. Browser storage is not a secure vault. Do not store sensitive information here.

A live service needs appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, access controls, retention limits, and a process for handling suspected incidents. Review the Security page for this template’s limitations.

7. Privacy questions and requests

Because this demo does not send its browser-local form data to a RoadRescue server, clearing the site’s browser data removes the demo records stored there. A live business should provide a monitored privacy contact and a clear process for access, correction, deletion, and other requests available under applicable law.

For general website questions, visit our contact page. Add a real privacy contact before collecting information from customers.

8. Policy updates

Update this policy whenever your data practices, technologies, or legal requirements change. Before publishing, add the responsible business’s legal name, effective date, contact details, and a clear explanation of how users will be notified of material changes.
