1. About this website and demo

RoadRescue provides website information about towing and roadside support. This project is a front-end demonstration: its forms, sample accounts, request tracking, and admin preview run locally in your browser. Submitting a request here does not contact a dispatcher, place a live order, or confirm that assistance is on the way.

Do not rely on a demo confirmation as a real booking. For actual assistance, contact your service provider directly and confirm that the request has been accepted.

2. Service requests and availability

Service descriptions and example coverage areas are general information. Actual availability depends on your location, vehicle, roadside conditions, equipment, weather, and provider capacity. Share accurate location and vehicle details so the provider can assess the request.

A request is not confirmed until the service provider directly confirms the service, destination (if towing), expected arrival window, and any conditions that apply.

3. Roadside safety and emergencies

This website and its providers are not a replacement for emergency services. If there is a collision, injury, fire, immediate threat, or unsafe traffic situation, contact local emergency services first and follow their instructions. Do not attempt repairs in a dangerous location.

4. Estimates, charges, and payment

Before authorizing work, ask the provider to explain the estimate, what it includes, and what could change the final amount. Charges may depend on distance, vehicle type, access, waiting time, equipment, and work performed. Confirm accepted payment methods and any cancellation or additional charges directly with the provider.

5. Independent service providers

Where a request involves an independent towing or roadside provider, that provider should confirm the service, timing, equipment, and charges with you. Before launching a real service, clearly describe your business’s role, provider relationships, complaint process, and any applicable service guarantees here.

6. Website use and information

Use this website lawfully and provide accurate information. Do not interfere with the site, attempt unauthorized access, or misuse its forms. Website content is provided for general information, may change, and is not a substitute for a provider’s assessment or professional advice.

7. Privacy and updates

This demo may save sample information and preferences in browser storage on your device. Review the Privacy Policy for more information. These terms should be updated if the website, services, or applicable requirements change; replace this template with your business’s effective date and update process before launch.

8. Questions about these terms

For website questions, visit our contact page. Before publication, add the responsible business’s full legal name, address, working contact details, and any jurisdiction-specific terms.
