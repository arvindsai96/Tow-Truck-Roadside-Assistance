Make the availability check useful

What to share when you contact a provider

A nearby region is only a starting point. Share a clear location and describe the vehicle and situation so the provider can confirm whether the requested service is practical.

  • Your exact location — address, road name, direction of travel, exit, or a nearby landmark.
  • Vehicle details — type, make/model if known, and any access or clearance constraints.
  • What happened — the issue, service you think you need, and whether the vehicle can move.
  • Safety conditions — traffic exposure, restricted access, weather, or other hazards.

If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
