Immediate Action Protocol

Emergency Roadside Safety: The First 5 Minutes

If your vehicle suddenly breaks down or develops a severe mechanical failure while driving, follow these four critical safety steps immediately.

1

Pull Over Safely

Signal early, ease off the accelerator, and steer smoothly toward the right shoulder or nearest exit ramp away from moving traffic lanes.

2

Maximize Visibility

Switch on your emergency hazard flashers immediately. If safe and off the highway, set reflective warning triangles 50 to 100 feet behind your vehicle.

3

Remain in Safe Position

On high-speed freeways, stay inside with seatbelts fastened. If exiting, always step out via passenger doors onto the grass or behind the guardrail.

4

Call 24/7 Dispatch

Call RoadRescue at (800) 555-0199 or request help online. Provide your exact mile marker, vehicle details, and current symptoms for rapid response.
