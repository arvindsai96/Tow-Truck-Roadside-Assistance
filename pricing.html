Clear & Understandable

What Determines Your Towing Cost?

We pride ourselves on zero hidden fees. Your exact quote is calculated using four straightforward, transparent criteria:

1. Vehicle Class & Weight

Standard passenger sedans require wheel-lift rigs, whereas heavy SUVs, full-size pickups, and electric vehicles require hydraulic flatbed transport.

2. Loaded Mileage

Mileage is measured strictly from where your car is hooked up to your designated repair shop, dealership, or home address. No deadhead charges.

3. Location & Access Difficulty

Vehicles parked on a standard shoulder are straightforward; vehicles trapped inside low-clearance underground garages or down ravines require specialty gear.

4. Specialized Equipment Needed

Locked steering wheels, missing keys, sheared ball joints, or missing wheels require specialized auxiliary dollies, skates, or dual-line winches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Pricing & Plans

Everything you need to know about roadside payment methods, insurance claims, and membership terms.

Will my auto insurance or credit card reimburse roadside fees?

Yes, most comprehensive auto insurance policies and premium credit cards include roadside assistance coverage. We supply full, itemized digital receipts with dispatch timestamps, service breakdowns, and loaded mileage so you can submit for fast, hassle-free reimbursement.

How is towing mileage calculated?

Towing mileage is calculated exclusively by "loaded miles" — the exact distance between your pickup breakdown location and your drop-off destination. We do not bill you for the miles our truck drives to reach you ("deadhead mileage").

Are there extra fees for night, weekend, or holiday service?

For annual plan members, service calls are covered 24/7/365 with zero after-hours surcharges. For one-time on-demand service, our dispatchers provide an all-inclusive binding quote upfront before you confirm, so you are never surprised by after-hours rates.

Can I buy an annual plan while my car is currently stranded?

Annual plans have a standard 24-hour waiting period to prevent immediate plan exploitation. However, we can dispatch help right now at our transparent on-demand rates and apply a $20 promotional discount toward an annual membership when you sign up.

What if my tow distance exceeds my membership allowance?

If your tow exceeds the free mileage allowance in your plan (e.g. 15 miles on Plus), you simply pay our heavily discounted member overage rate of $3.50 per additional mile, compared to the standard $4.50 – $5.00 non-member rate.

What happens if my car starts before the tow truck arrives?

If you notify us within 10 minutes of dispatch, cancellation is completely free of charge. If the operator arrives on scene before cancellation, a minor dispatch arrival fee ($35) applies to compensate the driver's fuel and time.

What payment methods are accepted on the scene?

You never have to carry cash. All RoadRescue operators carry encrypted mobile card readers that accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. You receive an instant digital receipt via SMS and email.

Are there extra fees for parking garage or low-clearance extraction?

Standard parking facilities with normal clearance have zero added charges. For low-clearance underground basements or tight garages requiring our specialized low-profile wheel-lift trucks or dollies, our dispatcher will quote any equipment fee upfront before arrival.

Does flat tire service include a replacement tire?

The standard $60 flat tire roadside fee covers mounting your vehicle's usable spare tire and testing tire pressure. If you do not have a usable spare, we can safely tow your vehicle to the nearest open tire shop or dispatch a mobile tire replacement unit.

Can family members or multiple vehicles share my membership?

Yes. On our Plus and Premier plans, you can register multiple household vehicles and authorized family drivers under a single account. Both the registered vehicles and household family members are protected whenever they travel.

Are electric vehicles (EVs) like Tesla covered, and does it cost more?

Yes, we provide full EV roadside coverage. Because all-wheel drive EVs cannot be towed with wheels rolling on the ground, we dispatch specialized hydraulic flatbed haulers with soft nylon wheel-straps and auxiliary 12V boosters at our standard rates with no hidden EV surcharges.

Can I choose which auto repair shop or destination my car is towed to?

Absolutely. You have 100% control over the destination. You can choose your trusted local mechanic, a certified dealership, an auto body shop, or your home driveway. Mileage is calculated directly from your breakdown spot to your designated address.
